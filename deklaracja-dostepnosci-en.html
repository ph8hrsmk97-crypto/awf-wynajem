Accessibility statement

Józef Piłsudski University of Physical Education in Warsaw is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with the Polish Act of 4 April 2019 on the digital accessibility of websites and mobile applications of public sector bodies.

This accessibility statement applies to the website wynajem.awf.edu.pl.

  • Date of publication of the website: [same date as in the Polish version]
  • Date of the last significant update: [same date as in the Polish version]

Compliance status

This website is partially compliant with the annex to the Act of 4 April 2019 on the digital accessibility of websites and mobile applications of public sector bodies, due to the non-compliances and exemptions listed below.

Non-accessible content

Content not covered by the regulations

The campus map is a PDF file published on awf.edu.pl. Maps are exempt from the digital accessibility requirements under Article 3(2)(4) of the Act of 4 April 2019 on the digital accessibility of websites and mobile applications of public sector bodies. We give the key information from the map as text: each venue on this website has its building number or a description of its location, and the campus address is ul. Marymoncka 34, 00-968 Warszawa. On request, we will describe the way to a chosen venue by e-mail or phone.

Preparation of this accessibility statement

  • Date of preparation of the statement: [same date as in the Polish version]
  • Date of the last review of the statement: [same date as in the Polish version]

We prepared this statement based on a self-assessment using a tool for the automatic detection of some digital accessibility errors (axe-core), a manual check of colour contrast and of operating the website with a keyboard. [Jeżeli koordynator przeprowadzi przegląd, zamienić na: „We prepared this statement based on a review carried out by the accessibility coordinator using commonly available tools for the automatic detection of some digital accessibility errors.”]

Features, limitations and other information

  • All functions of the website, including the hire cost calculator, can be used with a keyboard.
  • The website can be zoomed to 200% without loss of content. It adapts to the width of a phone screen.
  • The website automatically uses dark colours if this is set in your system.
  • If reduced motion is turned on in your system, the website turns off animations.
  • All photos of the venues have text alternatives that describe what the photo shows.
  • The website has a Polish and an English version (PL/EN switch in the page header).
  • Links to venue pages, virtual tours and documents in the Public Information Bulletin lead to awf.edu.pl, which has its own accessibility statement (in Polish).

Keyboard shortcuts

You can use your browser’s standard keyboard shortcuts on this website. The first press of Tab shows a “Skip to content” link that skips the page header. Esc closes the venue details and the enquiry form windows.

Feedback and contact information

You can report any digital accessibility problems with this website to Inga Maruszyńska-Małachowska — by e-mail at pelnomocnik.ozn@awf.edu.pl or by phone at +48 22 834 04 31 ext. 593.

Everyone has the right to request that this website or its elements be made digitally accessible.

When making such a request, please give:

  • your first name and surname,
  • your contact details (e.g. phone number, e-mail),
  • the exact address of the web page with the inaccessible element or content,
  • a description of the problem and the way of solving it that would be most convenient for you.

We will respond to your request as soon as possible, no later than 7 days after receiving it.

If this deadline is too short for us, we will let you know. We will give you a new date by which we will fix the errors you reported or provide the information in an alternative way. The new date will not be later than 2 months.

If we are unable to make the website or the content indicated in your request digitally accessible, we will offer you access to it in an alternative way.

Handling of requests and complaints related to accessibility

If, in response to your request for digital accessibility, we refuse to provide the accessibility you asked for and you disagree with the refusal, you have the right to file a complaint.

You also have the right to file a complaint if you do not agree to the alternative means of access that we offered in response to your request.

You can file a complaint by post or by e-mail:

  • Józef Piłsudski University of Physical Education in Warsaw, Office for Persons with Disabilities (Biuro ds. Osób z Niepełnosprawnościami),
  • Address: ul. Marymoncka 34, 00-968 Warszawa, Poland,
  • e-mail: pelnomocnik.ozn@awf.edu.pl.

You may find helpful information on the Polish government portal gov.pl (in Polish).

You can also inform the Commissioner for Human Rights (Rzecznik Praw Obywatelskich) about the situation and ask for intervention in your case.

Other information

Architectural accessibility

Campus of the Józef Piłsudski University of Physical Education in Warsaw — ul. Marymoncka 34, 00-968 Warszawa

The Main Building, the Faculty of Rehabilitation, the student halls of residence and the Administrative and Research Building have step-free entrances with permanent wheelchair ramps. The Women’s Hall of Residence has a lift for wheelchair users. The Men’s Hall of Residence has an internal lift or a stair platform lift next to the stairs.

Accessible toilets are on every floor of the Faculty of Rehabilitation Teaching Centre and the Administrative and Research Building, and on the ground floor of the Combat Sports Pavilion and the Main Building. In the Men’s Hall of Residence they are on every floor; in the Women’s and Rotacyjny halls of residence, on the ground floor.

There are designated parking spaces for people with disabilities near the building entrances. Some pavements on campus have dropped kerbs at pedestrian crossings and tactile paving at the edge of the roadway.

People with disabilities may enter the buildings and venues of the Józef Piłsudski University of Physical Education in Warsaw with an assistance dog, including a guide dog, under the applicable law. A person with an assistance dog should carry documents confirming the dog’s status and its current vaccinations. The dog should wear a harness. A muzzle or a lead is not required.

There are tram stops (lines 6, 15, 17, 27) and bus stops (lines 103, 121, 181, 755, N46) at the campus entrance. Stare Bielany metro station is a few hundred metres from the campus.

Sports venues for hire

The Józef Piłsudski University of Physical Education in Warsaw makes available sports venues used for teaching, sports events and recreation. The level of architectural accessibility varies between venues and depends on their construction and existing infrastructure.

[Do uzupełnienia – jak w wersji polskiej, na podstawie ankiety Działu Obiektów.]

Before hiring a venue, please contact the venue team (wynajem@awf.edu.pl) if you need information about accessible entrances and routes, changing rooms, showers and toilets, places for spectators with disabilities or organisational support.

Communication and information accessibility

The Józef Piłsudski University of Physical Education in Warsaw provides the services of a Polish Sign Language (PJM) interpreter on request. Please notify the Office for Persons with Disabilities at pelnomocnik.ozn@awf.edu.pl at least [3] working days before the planned contact with the university. The service can be provided on site or remotely (online). The service is free of charge.
